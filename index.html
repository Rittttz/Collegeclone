About The Institute

Welcome to Nalanda College of Engineering, Chandi

Nalanda College of Engineering (NCE), Chandi works under the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education, Government of Bihar. It was established in 2008 by the Hon'ble Chief Minister on the historic land where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment and where the world-renowned ancient Nalanda University once thrived.

The institute was initiated to cater to the growing demand for world-class technical education in eastern India. Starting with undergraduate courses in four classical streams, the college has rapidly expanded to include frontier disciplines such as Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Aeronautical Engineering, and Postgraduate (M.Tech) degrees.

Modern 35-acre green campus equipped with smart lecture halls & high-tech laboratories.
NBA Accredited Computer Science & Engineering department (2023–2025).
TEQIP-III funded institution with twinning arrangement and industry partnerships.
